# About Me

I am a **Senior Technical Writer with 8 years of experience** creating and maintaining technical documentation for SaaS and software products.

I work closely with SMEs, engineers, architects, QA teams, product owners, and cross-functional stakeholders to gather, validate, and transform complex technical information into clear, accurate, and user-focused documentation.
